Reusing an image or a video

To reuse an image or a video in any other way (website, presentation, training material, publication or social media), please ask us first. Write to info@proathlete.ca with the image or video you'd like to use and how you plan to use it. We're happy to help clubs, schools and coaches with educational uses.
